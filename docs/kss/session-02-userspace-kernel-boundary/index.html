Presenting on Teams

  1. Open the presenter view on your own display. Click Open audience window in its header (or use the card above).
  2. Move the audience window to your second display (or just keep it behind). Press F in it for fullscreen.
  3. In Teams, Share → Window → "… — audience". Never share your whole screen; the presenter view would leak.
  4. Drive from the presenter window: → / ← or a clicker. The audience window follows; the header dot turns green when it has caught up.
  5. For the live demo press B to blank the audience window, switch the Teams share to your terminal, then come back.
